# Dashboard

**Uses:** Cards, Charts, Progress, List rows, Table, Segmented control, Empty state, Button.

The first page of an app (App mode): the figures the user checks every day, the most important one leading.

- **Pieces:** a piece is there only when the user gives its data (Never invent values); the metrics row is always there. A piece whose data is empty for now keeps its card and its header, with the Empty state in place of its content (Empty state), and with no See all and no period control: the first-use case, its action a `tertiary` that opens the items' page ("Go to invoices"). With no data yet (the first day): the metrics row is one card holding one Empty state; the goal, chart and list cards keep their header above the Empty state, with no See all and no period control; the table card keeps the table's header, with the Empty state in place of its rows (Table, No rows) (user decision). Main pieces: the metrics row, the chart card. Side pieces: the goal card, the list card. The table card is the last row.
- **Grid:** the container is the page's main area. From a container of 1024px, three columns `1fr`: a main piece across two columns, a side piece across one, after it. The pieces are `space-8` (32px) apart, `space-6` below a screen of 640px (`sm`) (Cards, Card grid), in this order: metrics row, goal card, chart card, list card, table card. Each piece has `min-width: 0`, so a long word never widens its column.
- **Rows, from 1024px:** row 1 the metrics row, the goal card beside it; row 2 the chart card, the list card beside it; the table card last, across all three columns. With no chart card, the list card takes the goal card's place when there is no goal card, else row 2 alone. With a goal card and a chart card but no list card: row 1 the metrics row alone; row 2 the chart card, the goal card beside it. With a goal card and a list card, no chart card and 4 key figures, one grid holds every card, so they close on shared lines: columns 1 and 2 the leading card, then the key figures two per row; column 3 the list card beside the leading card and the first key figures' row, the goal card beside the last key figures' row with only its header and its bar; the table card last (user decision). A piece with nothing beside it takes the whole row.
- **Rows, below 1024px:** the pieces in the same order, side by side while each fits at the width of its content without wrapping, else under each other (user decision): `display: flex`, `flex-wrap: wrap`, each piece `flex: 1 1 max-content`; the table card always takes the whole row (`flex-basis: 100%`). No breakpoint per pair: the content decides.
- **Equal rows:** pieces side by side end at the same height, with no empty space under their content (user decision): the goal card's chart, the chart card's chart and the list card's rows take the space left; the metrics row's cards keep their height. An Empty state in a piece takes the space left, its content centred in it.
- **Row height:** a side piece takes the height of its row (Cards, Height): the main piece beside it sets it; with nothing beside it, its own content does.

## Metrics row

One grid inside the main cell: the leading card first, then 1 to 4 key figure cards. Its cards keep their own height: the row of the bento stretches the side card beside it, never these cards.

- **Grid:** its cards `space-8` apart, `space-6` below a screen of 640px (`sm`) (Cards, Card grid). Below a container of `kpi-pair` (360px) one column; from `kpi-pair` two columns `1fr`, the leading card across both, the key figures under it, two per row; a key figure with nothing beside it takes the whole row. The container is the dashboard's. From a dashboard of 1024px, when the metrics row has nothing beside it: three columns `1fr`, the leading card with a column 2 across two, each other card across one; when the leading card has no column 2, as many columns as cards (2 to 5, user decision). The cards side by side share three rows (header, figure, change line), so a header or a change line that wraps moves the same part of the cards beside it, and they end on one line.
- **Every card:** a card (Cards) with `space-6` padding. Header: the title in `title-lg` ("Revenue"), with no period after it: the leading card's period is the one of the whole row (user decision). Under the header, `space-2`: the figure in `display-sm` with tabular figures, its unit after it in `body-md` `mute`, on one baseline, `space-2` apart (`space-1` between lines when it wraps). Under the figure, `space-1`: the change line.
- **Change line:** a 16px Hugeicons arrow (`ArrowUpRight01` when the value grew, `ArrowDownRight01` when it fell, `ArrowRight01` when it is 0: "0 · 318"), then `space-2` after it, in `body-md` `mute`: the change, " · ", the previous period's value ("+4.2% · 11,980 €"), in the interface's number format. With no previous value the change line is not there. A change of a percentage is in points ("−0.4 points · 2.5%"). The arrow is centred on the text; direction never rests on color, so the text stays `mute` whether the change is good or bad.
- **Figure color:** the leading card's figure is `accent`; every other figure is `ink`.
- **Leading card:** the metric the user names as the most important; when the user names none, the agent asks before building ("Which number do you look at first?"). Its header is one row: the title ("Revenue") at the start, the period every figure of the row counts, the last complete one, at the end in `title-sm` `ink` ("September", when the current month is October), on one baseline, at least `space-4` apart; when they do not fit, the period wraps under the title, at the start, `space-1` under it. Under the header, two columns `1fr` `space-4` apart: column 1 the figure and the change line; column 2, the first the user's data allows: the comparison when it holds the figure's item beside its peers, else the trend. With fewer than 3 periods and no comparison, it has no column 2 and takes one column of the row, as a key figure, also when it is alone; the key figures follow it, two per row. Below a screen of 640px (`sm`) it has no column 2 (the trend or the comparison): one column, as a key figure (user decision).
- **Trend:** the last periods' values as a line in `chart-1`, `line-thick`, round joins and ends, oldest at the start, over its area in `chart-1` at 10% (Charts, Area under a line): the line runs from the lowest value, `line-thick` above the trend's bottom edge, to the highest at its top edge; the area reaches the bottom edge. No axes, no labels, no values; the SVG stretches to its box (`preserveAspectRatio="none"`), its line keeps 2px (Charts, Lines); the trend is `aria-hidden`, since the figure and the change line carry its values. It starts `space-2` under the header, at the figure's top, and ends at the change line's bottom, so it is as tall as figure + change, never taller: its SVG is `position: absolute`, `inset: 0` in its box, so the trend adds no height to its row. The area's bottom corners are `rounded-sm`. The clip that rounds them reaches `line-thick` above the trend and `space-1` past its end, so it never cuts the line. At the last value, a dot of 8px (`space-2`) in `chart-1` with a `line-thick` ring in `surface-card`, its centre on the line's end. Under `[dir="rtl"]` the trend is mirrored.
- **Comparison:** the figure's item beside its peers (one store among the stores), at most 8 items, as vertical bars (Charts), one per item, in the user's order: the figure's item in `chart-1`, the others in `hairline-strong` (Charts, Emphasis). They grow from a 1px `hairline` baseline, with no axes and no labels, spread across the trend's box with equal space between them. The values are read with the keyboard readout (Charts, Readout and Keyboard); its accessible name names the measure, the items and the period ("Revenue by store, September. Use the arrow keys to read the values."). With more than 8 items, the trend.
- **Glows:** the leading card holds the two glows (Glows), clipped by the card.
- **Entry:** the one choreography of App mode (user decision). Scroll reveal and Counting figures, written for Site sections, apply here as written: the metrics row and each piece is a section. The cards reveal one after the other, `duration-instant` (100ms) apart (Scroll reveal). Each figure counts while its card reveals (Counting figures). After the leading card's reveal the trend line draws on `duration-focal`, `ease-out`, then the dot fades in (`duration-slow`, `ease-linear`). After the leading card's reveal the comparison's bars grow from the baseline on `duration-focal`, `ease-out`, one after the other in their order, `duration-instant` apart; with reduced motion they fade in with the card. Reduced motion: as Counting figures and Chart entry (no count, no draw; the figures and the trend fade in).

## Chart card

A main piece (Rows): a card (Cards), `space-6` padding; from top: the header row; `space-6` under it the legend, then `space-4` under the legend the chart (`space-6` under the header row when there is no legend).

- **Header row:** the header at the start, the period Segmented control at the end, on one row aligned to their top, `space-4` apart; when the two do not fit on one row the control wraps under the header, at the start. Header: the title in `title-lg`, what is shown ("Orders"), no period after it, no subtitle (user decision).
- **Period control:** there only when the user's data holds the same series over two periods or more; 2 to 4 periods (Segmented control), one segment each, named by its length ("12 months", "8 weeks"), the longest first and selected; its accessible name "Period"; with 5 periods or more, a Select (Select) with the same name and order. Picking a period redraws the chart with that period's data, the line draws once (Chart entry), and the chart's accessible name changes to that period's.
- **Series:** a line chart (Charts), this period in `chart-1`; when the user's data holds the same period a year earlier, that line too, in `chart-2`. No area under the lines, no glows.
- **Legend:** with two series (Charts, Legend), `aria-hidden`, since the chart's accessible name names the series.
- **Chart:** It grows to fill the card's height, at least `chart` (200px). The y axis starts at 0, with round values (Charts, Axis values), the top one the first round value above the highest point (318 orders → 0, 100, 200, 300, 400), each with its 1px `hairline` grid line. Under the chart, one x label per point: the month's initial for months, the week's number for weeks. At the end of each line its end point; with one series, its last value (Charts, Direct labels).
- **Values:** (Charts, Values) the readout carries every value, with ← → Home End. Accessible name: the measure, the period and the series ("Orders, last 12 months, this year and last year. Use the arrow keys to read the values.").

## Goal card

A side piece (Rows): a card (Cards), `space-6` padding; its height follows Row height, and the line chart takes the space left.

- **Header:** the title in `title-lg`, the goal and its period ("October goal"); `space-1` under it the subtitle in `body-md` `mute`, the measure ("Revenue this month").
- **Bar:** `space-6` under the header, a Progress bar (Progress). Above the bar, one row: its label in `body-md` `ink` at the start ("Collected"), its value in `title-sm` with tabular figures at the end ("12,480 of 15,000 €"), `space-4` apart at least. The bar's accessible value is that same text.
- **Pairs:** `space-3` under the bar, two rows `space-3` apart, each a term in `body-md` `ink` at the start and its value in `title-sm` with tabular figures at the end, on one baseline: what is missing to the goal ("Missing", "2,520 €"), then the forecast for the end of the period ("Forecast at month end", "16,100 €"), only when the user gives it (Never invent values); without it, one row. Once the goal is reached, the first row reads "Over the goal" with the amount above it, and the bar stays full. A term and its value are a `<dl>` pair.
- **Chart:** `space-12` under the pairs, a line chart (Charts) of the period so far, one series in `chart-1`, no area, no legend. It grows to fill the card's height, at least `chart` (200px). The y axis starts at 0 and ends at the goal: its top grid line (Charts, Guides and axes) is the goal, its axis value the goal's figure (0, 5, 10, 15 thousand €). Once a point passes the goal, the axis ends at the first round value above the highest point, and the goal stays one of its grid lines, with its value. One x label per point, the day's number. At the end of the line its last value in `title-sm` `ink` with its unit (Charts, Direct labels). Accessible name: the measure, the period and the goal line ("Collected this month, day by day; the top line is the goal of 15 thousand €. Use the arrow keys to read the values.").
- **Entry:** the bar's value counts while the card reveals (Counting figures); after the reveal the bar fills on `duration-focal`, `ease-in-out` (Progress), and the line draws (Chart entry). Reduced motion: as Counting figures and Chart entry; the bar does not fill, it shows its value as the card fades in.

## List card

A side piece (Rows): a card (Cards), `space-6` padding; its height follows Row height.

- **Header:** the title in `title-lg`, what the items are ("Invoices to collect"); `space-1` under it the subtitle in `body-md` `mute`, which items are shown ("The next due dates"), never a count, since the rows shown follow the card's height.
- **Rows:** `space-6` under the header, the List rows (List rows) that fit in the card's height, at most 5, in the order the subtitle names (due date, soonest first): the first 3 always show, also when they make the card taller, and each next one only while it fits; the rows that do not fit are not shown, and the space left, less than one row, spreads between the rows, never less than `space-2` apart (List rows) (the content fits its tile). Each row is a clickable row to its item, with no leading element: the item's name, its detail ("Due 24 October"), and at the end its value and the arrow (List rows, Trailing).
- **See all:** a corner action (Corner actions): a `tertiary` icon button with the 20px `ArrowRight01`, its accessible label naming the list ("See all invoices to collect") and its tooltip "See all"; the header is its row. It opens the full list's page. When the row makes the card taller, the space goes between the rows.

## Table card

The last row of the dashboard, across every column: a card (Cards) holding a Table (Table), `space-3` padding above and below, `space-6` on the sides (Table, Container).

- **What it holds:** the 5 newest items of the list the user names ("Latest invoices"), newest first by their date; the full list lives on its own page, reached from the navigation; the card has no link to it.
- **Read-only:** no toolbar, no search, no row selection, no row menu, no pagination, no sorting (Table, On request only: the headers are plain text), no title block: the card starts with the table. Its columns, cells, loading and phone layout follow Table; on phones it scrolls inside the card.
- **Caption:** a `<caption>` hidden from view that names the table ("Latest invoices").
- **No rows:** as Table, No rows: the header stays, the Empty state in place of the rows, as in Pieces.
