# Comparison

**Uses:** Hero, Charts.

A section of the page (site.md Comparison): the product's figures beside one named competitor's.

- **Layout:** Side by side (`references/components/hero.md`), the text at the start and the chart at the end. The section has no title of its own above them.
- **Text:** the title in `headline-md` `ink`, which names the advantage the figures show ("Half the cost of the others"), never the word "Comparison"; its text in `body-lg` `body`; then one `primary` call to action, the last element of the column.
- **No panel:** the chart sits on the section's surface, never in a Card, a box or a border.
- **Chart:** Charts (`references/components/charts.md`), unchanged: the form follows its What the data does, the title says what is shown and the subtitle the conclusion. One figure each product → bars, ours in `chart-1` and the competitor in `hairline-strong` (Emphasis); a figure that grows with use → one line per product, ours in `chart-1` and the competitor in `chart-2`. The legend names the two products, never "us" and "them".
- **One competitor.** With two or more, the comparison is a Table on the pricing page.
