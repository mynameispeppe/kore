**Call to action**

- **Call to action:** one primary Button, a verb and an object ("Start free trial"), always the last at the end. It may repeat the page's main action (the hero's primary), with the same label or a different one: always visible, it is its own decision (The One Primary Rule).
- **Panel separator:** before the call to action, its line `space-4` from the visible text of the last row above it and `space-4` from the call to action below it. height `line-thin`; background `hairline`; margin-block calc(`space-4` − (`space-10` + `space-1` − the `body-lg` line-height) / 2) `space-4`.
- **Panel call to action:** last, at full width. With the avatar there is no call to action in the panel and no separator: the panel ends with its last row.
