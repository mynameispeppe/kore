**Search**

- **Search:** an icon-only tertiary Button with the Hugeicons `Search01` icon at `icon-sm`: its solid ring reads as large as the theme's 20px sun, whose rays are thin. Its label is "Search", shown as the tooltip. It opens the search dialog; ⌘K on macOS, Ctrl+K elsewhere, opens it from anywhere on the page and closes it when it is open.
- **Search dialog:** the Dialog, with no header and no close button: Escape or a tap on the scrim closes it, and focus returns to the Search button. Its body (padding `space-6`) holds the field, then the results.
- **Search field:** the Form field with the search icon (`Search01`, 16px, `mute`) at its start, `type="search"`, placeholder "Search pages and articles", its label "Search" visually hidden. It has focus when the dialog opens, empty. As wide as the result rows, with `space-4` under it before the first result: width auto; max-width none; margin-inline calc(`space-3` × −1); margin-block-end calc(`space-4` − `space-1`).
- **Results box:** List rows, each a link: title in `title-sm`, the description under it in `body-sm` `mute`, both on one line with an ellipsis; the category at the end in `body-sm` `mute`. The box keeps its height while the person types, and the rows' highlight is never clipped by the scroll. height calc(`space-48` + `space-32`); overflow-y auto; margin-inline calc(`space-3` × −1); padding-inline `space-3`.
- **Result rows:** they filter on every keystroke, on title, description and category, ignoring case. The first visible row is the active one; arrow down and up move it (wrapping at the ends, scrolling it into view); Enter or a click opens its page and closes the dialog. The hovered row, the focused one and the active one: background `surface-float-active`.
- **Search roles:** the field is a combobox (`aria-controls` the list, `aria-activedescendant` the active row, `aria-expanded="true"`); the list is a listbox of options; a visually hidden live region says the number of results ("4 results").
- **No results:** the Empty state in place of the list: the search icon in its circle, the title `No results for “<query>”` with the query in `fontWeight-strong`, the description "Check the spelling or try a shorter word." in `body-lg` `body`, and a tertiary Button "Clear search" that empties the field, shows every result again and puts focus back in the field.
