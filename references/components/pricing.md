# Pricing

**Uses:** Cards, Badge, Button, Segmented control.

A section of the page (site.md Pricing): the product's plans, each with its price, its call to action and what it includes.

- **Plans:** from 1 to 4, with the names, prices and items the user gives (Never invent values). Order: the user's; when the user gives none, cheapest first and the plan on request last. Each plan is a Plan card (`references/components/cards.md`).
- **Texts the user may not give:** the line under a plan's name says who the plan is for. It comes from the user like every text (Know the content before building); when the agent writes it, it does so from the plan's items, through humanizer when installed. A call to action without a label from the user reads "Choose [plan]", "Talk to sales" for a plan on request, in the interface's language.
- **Heading:** the section's title in `headline-md` `ink`, its text in `body-lg` `body` `space-1` (4px) under it, both centred in a column of at most 60 characters of `body-lg`, centred in the section. The plans start `space-12` (48px) under the last element above them.
- **Period:** only when every plan with a figure (not on request, not free) has both a monthly and a yearly price; otherwise each plan shows the one price the user gives. A Segmented control with two segments, monthly then yearly, monthly selected on load, centred `space-6` (24px) under the text; in the scroll reveal it rises with the heading. The monthly price is per month ("/month"), the yearly price is the year's total ("/year"). It switches every price of the section at once: the selected background slides as in Segmented control, the prices change with no View motion and no live region (the control's checked state already says it).
- **Yearly saving:** in the yearly segment, after its label, an inline Neutral Badge with the lowest saving among the paid plans, as a negative whole percent ("−16%"): 1 − yearly ÷ (12 × monthly), rounded down. A lowest saving of 0 or less shows no badge. The badge is `aria-hidden`; the segment's accessible name carries it ("Yearly, save 16%").
- **Plan content:**
  - **Free:** a plan the user gives at no cost shows "Free" in the figure's style, with no period.
  - **On request:** a plan the user prices on request shows "Custom" in the figure's style, with no period, and its call to action leads to a conversation.
  - **Rows:** every plan lists the same rows in the same order, so the cards of a row end with their content (user decision): every item the user gives for any plan, the first plan's items first, then each next plan's new items. A plan holds the items of the plans before it. A plan with the item: its row as in Plan card. A plan without it: the 16px Hugeicons `Cancel01` in `mute` in place of the tick, the text in `mute`, and a visually hidden "Not included: " before it. An item that names the same thing with another amount ("Up to 20 invoices a month", "Unlimited invoices") is one row, each plan with its own text; a plan before the first one holding it shows that first plan's text, not included.
- **Featured plan:** at most one, and only the one the user names. An inline Neutral Badge ("Most popular") on the name's row, at its end; when they do not fit on one row, the badge wraps under the name, at the start (user decision); the card carries the two glows (`references/rules/color.md`, Glows), clipped by the card. Without a named plan, no badge and no glows.
- **Arrangement:** the section's width is its content box. Cards side by side share the row equally, each within the Plan card's width, and the cards are centred; in one column every card is at the Plan card's widest; a section narrower than that makes the card full width. Gaps `space-8` (32px), `space-6` (24px) below 640px (`sm`).
  - 1 plan: one card at the Plan card's widest. Centred under the heading when the heading's `body-lg` text is at most 120 characters (2 lines of its 60-character column); when it is longer, beside it: the heading and the card are the two halves (Side by side, `references/components/hero.md`; Shape: Grid (text, Plan card)), starting at the same height instead of centred vertically. There the heading and the period control are on the start edge, not centred, and the card at the start edge of its half; below 1024px the heading comes first and the card `space-12` under it, on the start edge.
  - 2 or 3 plans: one row when the section is at least `n × plan-min + (n − 1) × space-8` wide (2 plans 608px, 3 plans 928px); below that, one centred column. Never some side by side and the rest below.
  - 4 plans: two rows of two when the section is at least `2 × plan-min + space-8` (608px) wide; below that, one centred column.
- **Zones aligned:** cards side by side start their name, the line under it, their price, call to action and list at the same height (subgrid), whatever the length of their texts.
