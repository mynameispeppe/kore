# Toast

- **Toast:** Replaces: `toast.toast`. The Glass recipe under a layer of the kind's vivid color, in place of the opaque fill: background linear-gradient(color-mix(in srgb, the kind's vivid color `mix-tint`, transparent)), `glass-background`; backdrop-filter `glass-backdrop`; box-shadow `glass-shadow`.
- **Icon circle:** Replaces: `toast.icon-circle`. As in the default, on the toast's glass with the layer of the kind's vivid color and the glass's edges, with no fill and no blur: background linear-gradient(color-mix(in srgb, the kind's vivid color `mix-tint`, transparent)); box-shadow inset 0 1px 0 `glass-edge-top`, inset 0 0 0 1px `glass-edge`; color the kind's glass icon color: `glass-icon-info`, `glass-icon-success`, `glass-icon-warning`, `glass-icon-danger`. Its icon: width `icon-xs`; height `icon-xs`.
- **Glass icon color:** each `glass-icon-*` is the kind's vivid color moved one tone at a time, as in Custom palette step 3, until it reaches 3:1 on the icon circle at the worst point behind the glass, the points of Text on glass. `typologies/glassmorphism/references/tokens.yaml` holds the result for Kore's palette; a Custom palette computes its own with `kore_palette.py --typology glassmorphism`.
