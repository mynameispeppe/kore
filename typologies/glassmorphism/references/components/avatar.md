# Avatar

- **Avatar:** Replaces: `avatar.avatar`. As in `references/components/avatar.md`, with the Glass recipe in place of background-color, background-image, box-shadow and outline: background `glass-background`; backdrop-filter `glass-backdrop`; box-shadow `glass-shadow`; outline none. An avatar inside a glass surface takes Glass inside glass.
- **Image avatar:** an image paints over the inset lines of its box-shadow, so the glass's top line and ring become its border: border `line-thin` solid `glass-edge`; border-top-color `glass-edge-top`.
- **Dot:** Replaces: `avatar.dot`. As in `references/components/avatar/presence.md`, with box-shadow `glass-inner-shadow`.
- **Dot in a card:** Replaces: `avatar.dot-in-a-card`. box-shadow `glass-inner-shadow`.
- **Group avatar:** Replaces: `avatar.group-avatar`. box-shadow `glass-shadow`; inside a glass surface `glass-inner-shadow`.
- **Group avatar in a card:** Replaces: `avatar.group-avatar-in-a-card`. box-shadow `glass-inner-shadow`.
