# Cards

- **Card:** Replaces: `cards.card`. As in `references/components/cards.md`, with the glass of 1 · surface: background `glass-fill`, `glass-fill-strong` on a photo; backdrop-filter blur(`glass-blur-card`) saturate(`glass-saturate`); outline-color `glass-border`; box-shadow `shadow-glass`. A card that Glass keeps solid, such as a plan card of Pricing or a card that holds a Table, a chart or a Figure, stays as the default.
- **Clickable hover:** Replaces: `cards.clickable-hover`. On mouse hover (hover: hover): box-shadow `shadow-glass-layered`; transform translateY(−`space-1`).
- **Clickable pressed:** Replaces: `cards.clickable-pressed`. transform none; box-shadow `shadow-glass`; transition-duration `duration-instant`.
