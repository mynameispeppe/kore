# Popover

- **Popover:** Replaces: `popover.popover`. As in `references/components/popover.md`, with the glass of 3 · floating: background `glass-fill-strong`; backdrop-filter blur(`glass-blur-float`) saturate(`glass-saturate`); outline-color `glass-border`; box-shadow `shadow-glass`. A popover that Glass keeps solid, such as one opened from a card, stays as the default.
- **Arrow:** Replaces: `popover.arrow`. As in the default, with background `glass-border`.
- **Arrow fill:** Replaces: `popover.arrow-fill`. As in the default, with background `glass-fill-strong`.
