# Badge

- **Badge:** Replaces: `badge.badge`. As in `references/components/badge.md`, with the Glass recipe in place of background-color, background-image, box-shadow and outline: background `glass-background`; backdrop-filter `glass-backdrop`; box-shadow `glass-shadow`; outline none. A badge inside a glass surface takes Glass inside glass.
- **Status badge:** Replaces: `badge.status-badge`. The Glass recipe under a layer of the kind's vivid color, with no ledge, as the Alert's container: background linear-gradient(color-mix(in srgb, the kind's vivid color `glass-badge-tint`, transparent)), `glass-background`; box-shadow `glass-shadow`; color `glass-badge-ink`. Inside a glass surface the layer lies over `glass-inner-background`, with box-shadow `glass-inner-shadow`.
- **Status badge text:** `glass-badge-ink` is `ink` moved one tone at a time, as in Custom palette step 3, until it reaches 4.5:1 on each kind's status badge, over the glass and over the inner glass, at the worst point behind the glass, the points of Text on glass. The layer's color follows what lies behind the glass; the word carries the meaning. `typologies/glassmorphism/references/tokens.yaml` holds the result for Kore's palette; a Custom palette computes its own with `kore_palette.py --typology glassmorphism`.
- **Count badge:** Replaces: `badge.count-badge`. As in `references/components/badge/count.md`, with background `ink`; backdrop-filter none; box-shadow `glass-inner-shadow`.
